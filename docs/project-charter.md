# HARMONI — Project Charter

## 1. Project Identity

**Project Name:** HARMONI

**Full Name:** Human–Automation Interaction for Safer and More Explainable Driving

**Project Type:** Human Factors + HCI + Experimental Research + Interactive Software Prototype

**Duration:** 6 weeks / 42 days

**Current Phase:** Day 1 — Research Foundation

---

## 2. Project Vision

HARMONI aims to investigate how intelligent automated systems can communicate their state, capabilities, limitations, and required actions to human operators.

The project focuses on the interaction between humans and automated driving systems rather than on developing the underlying autonomous-driving technology.

---

## 3. Primary Goal

To investigate whether explainable automation feedback can improve human understanding and intervention behaviour during Level-2 automated-driving scenarios.

---

## 4. Primary Research Question

> How does explainable automation feedback influence driver situation awareness and intervention behaviour during Level-2 automated-driving scenarios?

---

## 5. Target Population

The planned exploratory study will involve adults with driving experience.

The target sample is approximately 20 participants, subject to recruitment feasibility and final study methodology.

The sample will not be treated as representative of the entire driving population.

---

## 6. Experimental Conditions

### Control Condition

Conventional/minimal automation feedback.

The interface communicates system state and intervention requirements with limited contextual explanation.

### Experimental Condition

Explainable/contextual automation feedback.

The interface communicates:

- Current automation state
- Relevant detected condition
- System capability or limitation
- Reason for intervention
- Recommended human action

---

## 7. Independent Variable

### Automation Feedback Type

Two levels:

1. Conventional feedback
2. Explainable/contextual feedback

---

## 8. Dependent Variables

### Behavioural

- Reaction time
- Decision accuracy
- Intervention success
- Error rate

### Subjective

- Situation awareness
- Trust
- Perceived workload
- Confidence
- Perceived safety

---

## 9. Potential Control Variables

The study may account for:

- Driving experience
- Age group
- Familiarity with ADAS
- Previous exposure to automated-driving technologies
- General technology familiarity

The final control variables will be established during methodology development.

---

## 10. Human Factors Concepts

The project will investigate:

1. Situation awareness
2. Trust in automation
3. Appropriate reliance
4. Mental workload
5. Automation transparency
6. Human intervention
7. Human–machine interaction

---

## 11. Technical Objective

Develop a browser-based experimental environment capable of:

- Presenting controlled driving scenarios
- Simulating automation states
- Switching between interface conditions
- Triggering predefined events
- Recording participant actions
- Measuring reaction time
- Recording subjective responses
- Exporting experimental data

---

## 12. Research Objective

Produce an evidence-based evaluation of how different forms of automation feedback influence human interaction with an automated system.

---

## 13. Final Deliverables

- Literature review
- Research gap analysis
- Research proposal
- Experimental methodology
- Human Factors requirements
- Interface prototypes
- Browser-based simulation
- Scenario engine
- Event logging system
- Participant dataset
- Statistical analysis
- Research paper
- Research poster
- Portfolio case study
- Demonstration video

---

## 14. Scope Boundaries

HARMONI will not develop:

- Autonomous-driving perception algorithms
- Autonomous vehicle planning algorithms
- Vehicle control systems
- Real-world ADAS hardware
- Production automotive software
- Real-world vehicle testing

The automated-driving environment will be simulated so experimental conditions can be controlled consistently.

---

## 15. Research Integrity

All experimental results will be reported based on collected data.

The project will explicitly document:

- Unexpected findings
- Limitations
- Potential confounding factors
- Alternative interpretations
- Methodological weaknesses

No results will be fabricated or altered to produce a desired conclusion.

---

## 16. Definition of Done

HARMONI will be considered complete when:

- The research question is supported by relevant literature.
- The research methodology is clearly documented.
- Two experimental interface conditions are implemented.
- Controlled driving scenarios are implemented.
- Participant interactions are logged.
- A pilot study has been completed.
- The main exploratory study has been conducted where feasible.
- The collected data has been analysed appropriately.
- Findings and limitations are documented.
- The research paper is completed.
- The GitHub repository is professionally documented.
- The portfolio case study communicates the research process and outcomes.
