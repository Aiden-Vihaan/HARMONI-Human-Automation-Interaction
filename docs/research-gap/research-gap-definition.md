# HARMONI Research Gap Definition

## Day 10

---

# 1. Research Domain

Human–Automation Interaction

with particular emphasis on:

- Automation transparency
- Human-centric explanations
- Situation Awareness
- Mental Workload
- Trust
- Appropriate reliance
- Intervention performance

---

# 2. What Is Already Known?

Existing research provides evidence that:

1. Automation transparency can support Situation Awareness.
2. Transparency can improve operator performance in some contexts.
3. Transparency does not necessarily increase workload.
4. Mental workload can influence takeover performance.
5. Takeover timing affects Situation Awareness.
6. Trust influences reliance on automation.
7. Explanation effects on trust and acceptance are context-dependent.
8. Explanation timing and content matter.
9. Explanation length and modality remain important design variables.

---

# 3. What Is Less Clear?

The literature is less conclusive regarding the combined effect of:

Information Richness
        ↓
Situation Awareness
        +
Mental Workload
        ↓
Human Intervention
