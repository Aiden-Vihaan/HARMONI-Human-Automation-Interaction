# Day 09 — Evidence Synthesis

---

# Objective

Convert the literature collected during Days 1–8 into an evidence-based research framework.

---

# Five Core Constructs

1. Human–Automation Interaction
2. Situation Awareness
3. Mental Workload
4. Trust / Appropriate Reliance
5. Explainability / Transparency

---

# Strongest Evidence

## Finding 1

Automation transparency has evidence of improving Situation Awareness and performance in several experimental contexts.

## Finding 2

Transparency does not consistently produce a workload penalty.

## Finding 3

Mental workload can impair takeover performance when cognitive demand becomes high or non-optimal.

## Finding 4

Takeover timing affects Situation Awareness and takeover readiness.

## Finding 5

Trust influences reliance, but high trust is not necessarily equivalent to appropriate reliance.

## Finding 6

Explanations do not automatically improve trust or acceptance.

## Finding 7

Explanation timing, length, modality, and content remain important design variables.
