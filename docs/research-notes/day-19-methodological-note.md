# HARMONI — Day 19
## Methodological Note: Event-Based Measurement

---

# 1. Measurement Philosophy

HARMONI separates observation from interpretation.

The experimental software records observable events.

Analytical metrics are calculated afterward.

This reduces the risk of embedding analytical assumptions directly into the data-collection layer.

---

# 2. Example

The logger records:

COMMUNICATION_DISPLAYED
t = 12020 ms

PARTICIPANT_ACTION
