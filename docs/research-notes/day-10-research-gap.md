# Day 10 — Research Gap

## Main Decision

HARMONI will not attempt to study "explainable AI" as a broad topic.

The project will focus on a narrower Human Factors problem:

> How does increasing the richness of automation communication affect human understanding, cognitive workload, and intervention behaviour during automation limitations?

---

# Why This Is Stronger

A generic project would ask:

"Does explainability improve trust?"

HARMONI instead asks whether communication richness creates a trade-off between:

Situation Awareness
        ↕
Mental Workload
        ↕
Intervention
