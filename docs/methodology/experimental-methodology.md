# HARMONI Experimental Methodology

## Day 12

---

# 1. Study Design

HARMONI will use a controlled experimental study.

The experiment will compare different levels of automation communication richness.

---

# 2. Experimental Approach

Recommended initial design:

**Within-subject experimental design**

Each participant experiences multiple communication conditions.

---

# 3. Why Within-Subject?

Advantages:

- Participants act as their own control.
- Individual differences are reduced.
- Fewer participants may be required than in a fully between-subject design.
- Direct comparison between interface conditions becomes possible.

---

# 4. Main Independent Variable

Communication Richness

Four candidate levels:

1. Minimal
2. Contextual
3. Explanatory
4. Explanatory + Action Guidance

---

# 5. Primary Dependent Variables

## Situation Awareness

Measured using task-relevant questions and/or a validated SA methodology selected during final protocol development.

Potential dimensions:

- Perception
- Comprehension
- Projection

---

## Mental Workload

A validated subjective workload instrument will be used.

NASA-TLX is the current candidate.

Final selection will be justified using literature and feasibility.

---

# 6. Secondary Dependent Variables

## Intervention Performance

Potential measures:

- Reaction time
- Decision accuracy
- Correct intervention
- Missed intervention
- Inappropriate intervention

---

## Trust / Reliance

Potential measures:

- Post-condition trust
- Confidence
- Reliance intention
- Behavioural reliance

---

# 7. Experimental Scenario

The experiment will simulate a human supervising an automated system.

During normal operation:

Automation active
        ↓
User monitors
