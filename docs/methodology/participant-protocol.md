# HARMONI Participant Protocol

## Participant Session

---

# 1. Welcome

Thank you for participating in this study.

The study investigates how different forms of information provided by an automated system affect human understanding and decision-making.

---

# 2. What You Will Do

You will interact with a simulated automated system.

During the experiment, the system will operate automatically.

At certain points, the system may encounter a situation it cannot manage.

You will receive information from the system and will need to respond appropriately.

---

# 3. Important Instruction

Please respond based on the information and situation presented during each trial.

Do not try to guess which interface condition the researcher expects you to prefer.

There are no "preferred" answers.

---

# 4. Practice

You will complete practice trials before the actual experiment.

The practice trials are intended to familiarize you with the interface.

---

# 5. Experimental Trials

During each trial:

1. Automation will operate.
2. A scenario will develop.
3. A critical event may occur.
4. The system will communicate information.
5. You will respond.
6. You may be asked questions about the situation.
7. You may be asked to rate workload or confidence.

---

# 6. Withdrawal

You may stop participating at any time without needing to provide a reason.

---

# 7. Data

Your responses will be associated with a participant ID rather than your name whenever possible.

---

# 8. Final Instruction

Please focus on each trial individually.

Do not worry about whether your responses are correct.

The purpose of the experiment is to understand how people interact with different forms of automation communication.
