# HARMONI — Research Objectives

## Primary Objective

To investigate whether explainable automation feedback can improve human understanding and intervention behaviour during Level-2 automated-driving scenarios.

---

## Secondary Objectives

### Objective 1 — Situation Awareness

Investigate whether contextual automation feedback improves users' understanding of the current system state and surrounding situation.

### Objective 2 — Trust

Investigate how transparency about automation capabilities and limitations influences trust and confidence in the automated system.

### Objective 3 — Workload

Investigate how contextual information affects perceived mental workload during automation-critical scenarios.

### Objective 4 — Intervention

Measure whether different forms of automation feedback influence the speed and accuracy of human intervention.

### Objective 5 — Human Factors Design

Identify interface principles that can support appropriate human interaction with automated systems.

---

## Technical Objectives

The project will also aim to:

1. Build a controlled browser-based driving simulation.
2. Implement an automation-state model.
3. Implement multiple experimental scenarios.
4. Implement two interface conditions.
5. Develop an event-logging system.
6. Collect structured experimental data.
7. Develop an analysis pipeline in Python.
8. Produce reproducible visualisations and results.

---

## Portfolio Objective

Demonstrate an integrated capability across:

- Human–Computer Interaction
- Human Factors
- UX research
- Software engineering
- Experimental design
- Data analysis
- Scientific communication

The portfolio presentation should emphasize the research process and evidence rather than only the visual appearance of the interface.
