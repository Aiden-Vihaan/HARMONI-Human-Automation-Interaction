# HARMONI — Key Human Factors Concepts

## Purpose

This document defines the major Human Factors concepts currently considered relevant to HARMONI.

These definitions are provisional and will be refined using academic literature.

---

# 1. Situation Awareness

## Working Definition

Situation awareness refers to a person's understanding of relevant elements within a dynamic environment and their implications for future actions.

## HARMONI Relevance

A user interacting with automated driving should understand:

- What is happening around the vehicle
- What the automation is doing
- What the automation can handle
- What the automation cannot handle
- What action may be required next

## Potential Measurement

Possible approaches include:

- Situation-awareness questionnaires
- Scenario-based comprehension questions
- Decision accuracy
- State recognition accuracy
- Event interpretation accuracy

The final measurement method will be selected after literature review.

---

# 2. Trust in Automation

## Working Definition

Trust in automation refers to a person's willingness to rely on an automated system based on their beliefs about the system's capabilities, behaviour, and dependability.

## HARMONI Relevance

The interface may influence how much confidence users place in automation.

However:

> Higher trust does not necessarily mean better interaction.

The important concept is appropriately calibrated trust and reliance.

## Potential Measurement

Possible approaches include:

- Trust questionnaires
- Confidence ratings
- Reliance behaviour
- Willingness to follow system recommendations

---

# 3. Appropriate Reliance

## Working Definition

Appropriate reliance refers to relying on automation when it is capable of performing a task appropriately and intervening when automation cannot safely perform the task.

## HARMONI Relevance

This may be one of the most important behavioural outcomes in the project.
