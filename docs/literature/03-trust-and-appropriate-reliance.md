## Trust, Reliance, and Human–Automation Interaction

---

# 1. Purpose

Trust is one of the most widely discussed concepts in Human–Automation Interaction.

When people interact with an automated system, their level of trust can influence whether they:

- Use the system
- Follow its recommendations
- Monitor it
- Ignore it
- Intervene
- Continue relying on it after a failure

However, trust alone does not necessarily indicate successful human–automation interaction.
