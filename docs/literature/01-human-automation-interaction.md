## Purpose

This literature section establishes the theoretical foundation for understanding how humans interact with automated systems.

HARMONI investigates human interaction with automated driving systems, so understanding the relationship between human operators and automation is necessary before designing the experimental interface.

This document will be expanded as relevant academic literature is reviewed.

# 1. Why Human–Automation Interaction Matters

Automation changes the role of the human operator.

In a conventional manually controlled system, the human directly performs most of the task.

In an automated system, some functions are transferred to the system.

This creates a new interaction relationship:
