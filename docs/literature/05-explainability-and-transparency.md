# Literature Review 05 — Explainability and Automation Transparency

## Project

HARMONI

## Research Area

Explainability, Transparency, and Human–Automation Interaction

---

# 1. Purpose

Human users interacting with automated systems may need more than a simple indication of what the system is doing.

They may also need to understand:

- What the system is doing
- Why the system is doing it
- What the system can currently do
- What the system cannot do
- What changed
- What is likely to happen next
- Whether human intervention is required

This creates an important distinction between:

System Information
        ↓
Transparency
        ↓
Explainability
