# Literature Review 02 — Situation Awareness

## HARMONI Research Area

Situation Awareness in Human–Automation Interaction

---

# 1. Purpose

Situation Awareness (SA) is a central Human Factors concept for HARMONI.

When humans supervise or interact with automation, effective intervention may depend on whether the human understands:

- What is happening
- What the automated system is doing
- What the surrounding environment contains
- What the system can and cannot handle
- What is likely to happen next
- What action may be required

This document establishes the theoretical foundation for investigating Situation Awareness within HARMONI.

The concepts documented here are preliminary and will be refined through academic literature.

---

# 2. Working Definition

Situation Awareness can be broadly understood as the human's understanding of relevant elements of a dynamic environment, their current state, and their implications for future actions.

A simplified representation is:

Environment
     ↓
Information
     ↓
Human perception
     ↓
Understanding
     ↓
Prediction
     ↓
Action
