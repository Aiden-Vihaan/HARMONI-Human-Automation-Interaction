# 4. Transparency Evidence

A systematic review of experimental transparency studies identified 17 controlled experiments.

The review found an overall trend toward beneficial effects of transparency.

However, effects differed depending on:

- Task
- Agent type
- Interaction type
- Level of transparency
- Integration of information into the primary display

The review reported promising effects on Situation Awareness and operator performance without a consistent workload penalty in some supervisory and proposal-response contexts.

This is important because it challenges the simplistic assumption:

> More information necessarily creates more workload.

Instead:

> The effect of information depends on what information is provided and how it is integrated.

# 5. Automated Vehicle Explanation Evidence

A recent systematic review examined human-centric explanations in automated vehicles.

The review included 59 relevant studies.

Important findings included:

- Explanations that clarify reasoning can be more useful than descriptions of actions.
- Detailed explanations do not automatically increase trust or acceptance.
- Explanation timing matters.
- Explanations provided before actions may be beneficial.
- The optimal timing remains uncertain.
- Explanation length remains an unresolved design issue.
- Modality and narrative perspective can influence outcomes.
- More real-world research is required.

This provides strong evidence that explanation design should be treated as a multidimensional problem.

# 6. Situation Awareness Evidence

Situation Awareness is repeatedly identified as important during automation transitions.

Automated-driving studies indicate that users may need to reconstruct the driving situation when automation can no longer handle an upcoming event.

Takeover-request lead time can influence Situation Awareness.
