# HARMONI — Literature Review Strategy

## Purpose

The literature review will establish the scientific foundation of HARMONI.

The objective is not to collect papers that simply support the project's initial assumptions.

Instead, the review will identify:

- What is already known
- How previous researchers have studied the problem
- Which measurements have been used
- Which methodologies are appropriate
- What limitations exist
- Where a meaningful exploratory research gap may exist

---

# 1. Primary Research Areas

The literature review will focus on:

1. Human–automation interaction
2. Human Factors in automated driving
3. Situation awareness
4. Trust in automation
5. Appropriate reliance
6. Mental workload
7. Automation transparency
8. Explainable automation
9. Explainable AI
10. Automated-driving interfaces
11. Takeover/intervention behaviour
12. Driver monitoring
13. Human–machine interface design
