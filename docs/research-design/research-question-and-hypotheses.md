# HARMONI Research Question and Hypotheses

## Day 11

---

# 1. Final Research Question

> How does the richness of automation communication during a critical system limitation affect human Situation Awareness, mental workload, and intervention performance?

---

# 2. Secondary Research Question

> Does increasing communication richness improve Situation Awareness at the cost of increased mental workload?

---

# 3. Exploratory Research Question

> How does communication richness influence trust and reliance on automation during a critical limitation?

Trust/reliance will be treated as a secondary exploratory outcome rather than the primary outcome.

---

# 4. Research Objectives

## Objective 1

Determine whether communication richness affects Situation Awareness.

## Objective 2

Determine whether communication richness affects perceived mental workload.

## Objective 3

Determine whether communication richness affects intervention performance.

## Objective 4

Explore whether communication richness influences trust/reliance.

## Objective 5

Identify whether there is a communication level that provides useful information without excessive cognitive demand.

---

# 5. Independent Variable

## Communication Richness

The experimental manipulation will vary the amount and functional type of information provided by the automation.

---

# 6. Proposed Experimental Levels

## Level 1 — Minimal State

Provides only the system state.

Example:

AUTOMATION LIMITED

TAKE CONTROL
